Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Unified Service Intelligence Platform (USIP)
Version: 1.0Status: Development-Ready DraftImplementation Approach: Conventional rule-based platform
1. Document Overview
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the requirements for the design, development, testing, and deployment of the Unified Service Intelligence Platform (USIP).
USIP is a centralized, multi-brand, multi-provider platform for managing home-appliance service requests, complaints, technician assignments, warranty and AMC records, spare-parts inventory, job cards, billing, training, feedback, escalations, and service analytics.
The system will support appliances including, but not limited to:
Air conditioners.
Refrigerators.
Microwaves.
Chimneys.
Dishwashers.
Hobs.
Cooktops.
Cooking ranges.
Ovens.
Washing machines.
Water purifiers.
Coffee machines.
Other consumer home appliances.
1.2 Business Problem
The home-appliance after-sales service industry is fragmented and inconsistent. Consumers typically:
Search for brand toll-free numbers on search engines and third-party websites.
Contact unauthorized or fraudulent service providers.
Receive unverified technicians at their premises.
Face overcharging, poor repairs, counterfeit parts, and safety risks.
Struggle to track complaints and service requests.
Experience silent closure of complaints without proper resolution.
Appliance companies and service providers face:
Poor visibility of field operations.
Inefficient complaint management.
Inconsistent technician performance.
Weak control over spare parts and pricing.
Limited ability to measure service quality and SLAs.
1.3 Proposed Solution
USIP will act as a unified service management ecosystem connecting:
Consumers.
Appliance manufacturers (brands).
Authorized service centers.
Third-party service providers.
Field technicians.
Spare-parts suppliers.
Platform administrators.
The platform will:
Provide a single verified entry point for service requests and complaints.
Route requests only to verified brands and authorized service partners.
Enable end-to-end tracking and transparent status updates.
Enforce structured escalation paths.
Use configurable business rules, verification controls, and audit logs to reduce fraud and improve accountability.
USIP will be implemented as a conventional software platform using:
Configurable workflows.
Role-based access control.
APIs for brand integrations.
Notifications and dashboards.
Immutable audit trails.
No autonomous AI agents or Agentic AI components will be used in the initial release.
1.4 Objectives
The system shall:
Provide one centralized platform for appliance service requests and complaints.
Improve transparency across the service lifecycle.
Allow customers to track requests in real time.
Verify organizations, service centers, and technicians.
Reduce unauthorized technician visits and brand impersonation.
Standardize job assignment, pricing, billing, and complaint closure.
Manage warranty and AMC information.
Improve technician scheduling and utilization.
Track spare-parts availability and usage.
Provide company-level and platform-level reporting.
Support escalation when service-level deadlines are missed.
Preserve immutable service and communication records.

2.3 Product Boundaries
USIP coordinates service operations but is not the legal manufacturer, warranty provider, or employer of technicians unless a separate contractual arrangement exists.
Each appliance brand remains responsible for:
Its warranty policies.
Product safety instructions.
Authorized service decisions.
Spare-part authenticity.
Technician authorization.
Final commercial liability for brand-related services.

6. Functional Requirements
6.1 User Registration and Authentication
FR-AUTH-001: The system SHALL allow consumers to register using mobile number or email.
FR-AUTH-002: The system SHALL verify mobile numbers using one-time passwords.
FR-AUTH-003: The system SHALL support password reset through an approved verification process.
FR-AUTH-004: The system SHALL support optional social login if approved by the product owner.
FR-AUTH-005: The system SHALL support multi-factor authentication for administrators, brands, and service providers.
FR-AUTH-006: The system SHALL lock or rate-limit repeated failed login attempts.
FR-AUTH-007: The system SHALL record login, logout, failed-login, and account-lock events.
FR-AUTH-008: The system SHALL allow users to view and update their profile information.
FR-AUTH-009: The system SHALL require consent before collecting optional personal information.
6.2 Organization Onboarding
FR-ORG-001: The system SHALL allow appliance brands and service providers to submit onboarding applications.
FR-ORG-002: The system SHALL collect legal name, business address, tax details where applicable, contact details, and authorized representative details.
FR-ORG-003: The system SHALL support document upload for organization verification.
FR-ORG-004: Platform administrators SHALL be able to approve, reject, suspend, or request additional information.
FR-ORG-005: The system SHALL record the complete organization-verification history.
FR-ORG-006: Suspended organizations SHALL not receive new service requests.
FR-ORG-007: The system SHALL display verification status to authorized users.
6.3 Appliance Catalog
FR-CAT-001: Authorized brand users SHALL be able to create and manage appliance categories.
FR-CAT-002: The system SHALL store brand, product family, model, serial-number format, warranty rules, and service instructions.
FR-CAT-003: The system SHALL allow consumers to add appliances manually.
FR-CAT-004: The system SHALL allow consumers to upload proof of purchase.
FR-CAT-005: The system SHALL support appliance records without a serial number, subject to manual verification.
FR-CAT-006: The system SHALL maintain an appliance history of service requests, repairs, parts, and warranty events.
6.4 Service Request Creation
FR-TKT-001: Consumers SHALL be able to create service requests through web and mobile interfaces.
FR-TKT-002: The service-request form SHALL include appliance category, brand, model, serial number when available, issue type, description, location, and preferred appointment slot.
FR-TKT-003: The system SHALL support service types including installation, repair, maintenance, inspection, warranty claim, AMC service, and complaint.
FR-TKT-004: The system SHALL allow attachments including photographs, videos, invoices, and documents.
FR-TKT-005: The system SHALL generate a unique service-request number.
FR-TKT-006: The system SHALL display the selected brand and responsible service organization.
FR-TKT-007: The system SHALL show whether the appliance is covered by warranty or AMC when relevant information is available.
FR-TKT-008: The system SHALL allow users to save a request as a draft.
FR-TKT-009: The system SHALL prevent duplicate submission caused by repeated button clicks.
FR-TKT-010: The system SHALL provide a confirmation screen before submission.
FR-TKT-011: The system SHALL notify the consumer after successful submission.

6.6 Routing and Assignment
FR-ROUTE-001: The system SHALL route a service request to the selected brand when the brand is verified and active.
FR-ROUTE-002: The system SHALL route a request to an approved service center based on service area.
FR-ROUTE-003: The system SHALL consider technician skill, availability, distance, workload, and authorization.
FR-ROUTE-004: The system SHALL prevent assignment to suspended or expired technicians.
FR-ROUTE-005: The system SHALL allow manual reassignment by authorized coordinators.
FR-ROUTE-006: The system SHALL record the reason for manual reassignment.
FR-ROUTE-007: The system SHALL notify the assigned service provider and consumer.
FR-ROUTE-008: The system SHALL create an exception when no eligible provider is available.
FR-ROUTE-009: Exception cases SHALL be placed in an administrative queue for resolution.
6.7 Scheduling
FR-SCH-001: Service providers SHALL be able to define technician working hours.
FR-SCH-002: The system SHALL display available appointment slots.
FR-SCH-003: The system SHALL prevent double-booking.
FR-SCH-004: Consumers SHALL be able to request rescheduling.
FR-SCH-005: Authorized service staff SHALL be able to approve or reject rescheduling requests.
FR-SCH-006: The system SHALL record appointment history.
FR-SCH-007: The system SHALL send appointment reminders.
FR-SCH-008: The system SHALL record technician arrival and departure times.
6.8 Technician Verification and Doorstep Safety
USIP shall provide a verification mechanism to reduce unauthorized technician visits.
FR-TECH-001: Every active technician SHALL have a unique platform identity.
FR-TECH-002: The system SHALL store technician name, photograph, organization, service areas, skills, certifications, and authorization status.
FR-TECH-003: The system SHALL assign a technician only if the technician is active and approved.
FR-TECH-004: The system SHALL generate a service-specific verification code before the appointment.
FR-TECH-005: The code SHALL be valid only for the relevant service request and defined appointment period.
FR-TECH-006: The consumer SHALL be able to verify the technician using the technician ID, OTP, QR code, or authenticated application.
FR-TECH-007: The system SHALL show the technician’s name, photograph, organization, and service-request number to the consumer.
FR-TECH-008: The technician SHALL not be allowed to start the job until the arrival verification is completed, except through an authorized exception process.
FR-TECH-009: The consumer SHALL be able to report a mismatch or suspected fraud.
FR-TECH-010: A verification failure SHALL notify the service provider and platform support team.
FR-TECH-011: The system SHALL log all verification attempts.
FR-TECH-012: The system SHALL allow the consumer to cancel or pause the visit when the technician identity does not match the displayed information.
Doorstep Verification Workflow
The system assigns an approved technician.
The consumer receives technician details.
The technician arrives and opens the assigned job.
The system generates or validates a service-specific OTP or QR code.
The consumer compares the technician’s identity with the platform record.
The consumer confirms the visit.
The system records arrival time and verification evidence.
The technician performs only the approved service.
The consumer confirms completion or raises a dispute.
The verification process shall not be treated as a substitute for physical safety precautions. The platform shall advise consumers not to share unrelated passwords, banking credentials, or unnecessary personal documents.

FR-WAR-002: The system SHALL store warranty start date, end date, coverage type, exclusions, and supporting documents.
FR-WAR-003: The system SHALL allow AMC contract creation and renewal.
FR-WAR-004: The system SHALL notify consumers before AMC or warranty expiry.
FR-WAR-005: The system SHALL identify requests requiring warranty verification.
FR-WAR-006: Authorized staff SHALL be able to approve or reject warranty claims.
FR-WAR-007: Warranty decisions SHALL include a reason and timestamp.
FR-WAR-008: The system SHALL retain warranty history for each appliance.
6.10 Diagnosis and Estimate

FR-DIAG-002: Technicians SHALL be able to record error codes, inspection results, and recommended actions.
FR-DIAG-003: Technicians SHALL be able to upload photographs and videos.
FR-DIAG-004: The system SHALL support configurable diagnosis templates by appliance category.
FR-DIAG-005: The system SHALL generate an estimate based on approved labor, travel, and parts pricing.
FR-DIAG-006: Consumers SHALL receive estimates before chargeable work begins.
FR-DIAG-007: Consumers SHALL be able to approve or reject estimates.
FR-DIAG-008: Additional work SHALL require separate approval.
FR-DIAG-009: A technician SHALL not mark a paid repair as complete without recording the customer’s approval.
6.11 Job Cards
FR-JOB-001: The system SHALL create a digital job card for every service visit.
FR-JOB-002: A job card SHALL include service-request number, appliance information, technician, visit date, diagnosis, work performed, parts, charges, photographs, and customer confirmation.
FR-JOB-003: The technician SHALL submit the job card after completing the work.
FR-JOB-004: The consumer SHALL be able to review the job card.
FR-JOB-005: The consumer SHALL be able to accept the job card, reject it, or raise a dispute.
FR-JOB-006: Approved job cards SHALL not be edited without a controlled correction process.
FR-JOB-007: All corrections SHALL be audit logged.
6.12 Inventory Management
FR-INV-001: Service providers SHALL be able to create inventory locations.
FR-INV-002: The system SHALL track part number, description, brand, compatibility, batch number, quantity, cost, and status.
FR-INV-003: The system SHALL support stock-in, stock-out, transfer, return, damaged, and reserved statuses.
FR-INV-004: Parts used on a job SHALL be linked to the job card.
FR-INV-005: The system SHALL prevent use of unavailable or blocked parts.
FR-INV-006: The system SHALL provide low-stock notifications.
FR-INV-007: The system SHALL support serial-number tracking for controlled parts.
FR-INV-008: Inventory adjustments SHALL require authorization and a reason.
FR-INV-009: The system SHALL support counterfeit or authenticity warnings where brand data is available.
6.13 Billing and Payments
FR-BILL-001: The system SHALL calculate charges according to configured pricing rules.
FR-BILL-002: Charges may include inspection, labor, travel, parts, taxes, discounts, and other approved fees.
FR-BILL-003: Consumers SHALL see a cost breakdown before payment.
FR-BILL-004: The system SHALL generate invoices for completed chargeable services.
FR-BILL-005: The system SHALL support online payment integration where enabled.
FR-BILL-006: The system SHALL record cash or offline payment references where permitted.
FR-BILL-007: The system SHALL support payment status values such as pending, successful, failed, refunded, and disputed.
FR-BILL-008: Refunds SHALL require authorization.
FR-BILL-009: Technicians SHALL not be allowed to alter approved pricing without permission.
FR-BILL-010: The system SHALL send payment and invoice notifications.
6.14 Customer Feedback and Complaint Reopening
FR-FBK-001: The system SHALL request feedback after service completion.
FR-FBK-002: Consumers SHALL be able to rate service quality, technician behavior, pricing transparency, and resolution quality.
FR-FBK-003: Consumers SHALL be able to submit written comments.
FR-FBK-004: Negative feedback SHALL create a review task.
FR-FBK-005: Consumers SHALL be able to reopen a request within a configurable period.
FR-FBK-006: Reopened requests SHALL retain the original service history.
FR-FBK-007: Organizations SHALL not be able to delete customer feedback.
FR-FBK-008: Platform administrators SHALL be able to hide content that violates published usage rules while retaining the original record for audit.
6.15 Escalation Management
FR-ESC-001: The system SHALL support configurable escalation levels.
FR-ESC-002: Escalation rules SHALL be based on time, status, severity, customer complaint, or SLA breach.
FR-ESC-003: The system SHALL notify the responsible service manager when escalation conditions are met.
FR-ESC-004: The system SHALL escalate unresolved cases to brand-level administrators.
FR-ESC-005: Platform administrators SHALL be able to escalate cases to a dispute-resolution queue.
FR-ESC-006: Each escalation SHALL have an owner and due date.
FR-ESC-007: The system SHALL maintain escalation history.
FR-ESC-008: Escalated cases SHALL not be closed without an authorized resolution note.
FR-ESC-009: Consumers SHALL be notified when a case is escalated or resolved.

USIP shall show the consumer a transparent status.
USIP shall not silently discard the request.
8. Fraud Prevention and Verification Controls
USIP shall use rule-based controls and verified data sources. It shall not claim to guarantee fraud prevention.
8.1 Official Channel Controls
The platform SHALL display verified brand contact details.
Only authorized brand administrators SHALL be allowed to change official contact details.
Changes to official numbers, websites, or service channels SHALL require approval.
The system SHALL show verification status and last verification date.
The system SHALL maintain a history of changes.
Users SHALL be warned against using unverified phone numbers or payment links.
8.2 Organization and Technician Controls
Organizations SHALL undergo verification before receiving consumer jobs.
Technicians SHALL have unique identities.
Technician profiles SHALL include photograph and organization affiliation.
Technician access SHALL expire when authorization or certification expires.
The system SHALL detect duplicate technician identity records for administrative review.
Suspended technicians SHALL not be assignable.
Doorstep verification SHALL be required for configured service types.
8.3 Suspicious Activity Rules
The system may flag activity when:
A technician attempts repeated failed verifications.
A provider generates abnormal cancellation patterns.
A technician performs jobs outside approved areas.
A service provider repeatedly changes invoice amounts.
A user reports identity mismatch.
A provider receives unusually high fraud complaints.
A payment link is not generated by the platform.
A technician attempts to access unrelated customer data.
Flagged events SHALL be reviewed by authorized staff. Automatic suspension of an organization shall require configured policy and approval unless an emergency security rule applies.
9. Non-Functional Requirements
9.1 Performance
NFR-PERF-001: Standard page requests SHOULD return within 2 seconds under normal load.
NFR-PERF-002: Service-request submission SHOULD complete within 3 seconds excluding external gateway delays.
NFR-PERF-003: The system SHALL provide asynchronous processing for document uploads, notifications, and external integrations.
NFR-PERF-004: The system SHALL support configurable load targets based on the approved capacity plan.
NFR-PERF-005: Reporting queries SHALL not materially affect transactional performance.
NFR-PERF-006: API rate limits SHALL protect the platform from abuse.
The 2-second target applies to normal user-interface and rule-processing operations. It shall not be treated as a requirement for every external API, payment gateway, file upload, or report.
9.2 Availability and Reliability
NFR-REL-001: The production platform SHALL target at least 99.5% monthly availability, excluding approved maintenance.
NFR-REL-002: The platform SHALL use health checks and service monitoring.
NFR-REL-003: Critical services SHALL support automatic restart or failover.
NFR-REL-004: The system SHALL not lose a successfully committed service request.
NFR-REL-005: Background jobs SHALL support retry and dead-letter processing.
NFR-REL-006: The platform SHALL provide operational alerts for critical failures.
9.3 Scalability
The system shall support horizontal scaling for:
API services.
Notification processing.
Document processing.
Reporting workloads.
Integration workers.
Database read operations.
The exact capacity targets shall be approved before performance testing.
9.4 Security
NFR-SEC-001: All network communication SHALL use encrypted transport.
NFR-SEC-002: Sensitive data SHALL be encrypted at rest.
NFR-SEC-003: Passwords SHALL be stored using a strong, approved one-way hashing method.
NFR-SEC-004: Administrative accounts SHALL require multi-factor authentication.
NFR-SEC-005: The system SHALL implement role-based access control.
NFR-SEC-006: The system SHALL protect against common web vulnerabilities.
NFR-SEC-007: The system SHALL validate and sanitize all user input.
NFR-SEC-008: File uploads SHALL be scanned and restricted by type and size.
NFR-SEC-009: Secrets SHALL not be stored in source code.
NFR-SEC-010: Security events SHALL be logged and monitored.
NFR-SEC-011: Sensitive information SHALL be masked in logs.
NFR-SEC-012: Session tokens SHALL expire and support revocation.
NFR-SEC-013: The system SHALL support security testing before production release.
9.5 Privacy and Data Protection
The platform shall comply with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements in its operating jurisdictions.
The platform shall:
Collect only necessary personal data.
Provide privacy notices.
Record consent where required.
Provide a method to request correction of personal data.
Define retention periods.
Restrict access to personal information.
Support deletion or anonymization where legally permissible.
Process data according to contractual and legal responsibilities.
Maintain records of data-access requests.
Avoid exposing consumer contact details unnecessarily to technicians.
The final implementation shall identify the applicable local laws before production deployment. GDPR compliance alone shall not be assumed to satisfy all local requirements.
9.6 Auditability
NFR-AUD-001: The system SHALL record creation, modification, assignment, status changes, approvals, payments, verification events, and escalations.
NFR-AUD-002: Audit records SHALL include actor, timestamp, organization, action, previous value where applicable, new value, and source.
NFR-AUD-003: Users SHALL not be able to delete audit records.
NFR-AUD-004: Audit records SHALL be protected from unauthorized modification.
NFR-AUD-005: Administrators SHALL be able to search audit records based on approved permissions.
NFR-AUD-006: The system SHALL support export of audit evidence for investigations.

12. Business Rules
A request SHALL belong to one consumer and one appliance record.
A technician SHALL not be assigned to a request outside the technician’s approved service scope unless authorized.
A service request SHALL not be closed without a closure reason.
A chargeable repair SHALL require estimate approval before work begins.
A technician SHALL not edit an approved invoice without authorization.
A suspended organization SHALL not receive new requests.
A technician with expired credentials SHALL not be assignable.
A customer complaint shall remain visible in the customer’s history even after closure.
An escalated request SHALL have an assigned escalation owner.
Every status change SHALL be recorded in the history.
A brand may define brand-specific service and warranty rules.
Platform administrators may configure general policies but shall not override contractual brand decisions without authorization.
13. Key Workflows
13.1 Consumer Service Request Workflow
Consumer logs in.
Consumer selects an existing appliance or adds a new appliance.
Consumer selects service type.
Consumer describes the issue and uploads supporting information.
System validates mandatory fields.
System checks available brand and service coverage.
System creates a service-request number.
System routes the request to an approved brand or service provider.
Consumer receives confirmation.
Service provider reviews and accepts the request.
Appointment is scheduled.
Technician is assigned.
Consumer receives technician information.
Technician completes doorstep verification.
Technician performs inspection or repair.
Technician submits diagnosis, parts, estimate, and job details.
Consumer approves the estimate if required.
Technician completes the service.
Consumer reviews the job card.
Consumer confirms completion or raises a dispute.
Invoice and payment are recorded.
Feedback is collected.
Request is closed or escalated.
13.2 Complaint Escalation Workflow
System detects an SLA breach or consumer raises an escalation.
System creates an escalation record.
Escalation is assigned to the responsible service manager.
Consumer receives escalation confirmation.
Service manager records investigation and action.
If unresolved, the case moves to the next escalation level.
Final resolution is recorded.
Consumer is notified.
Consumer confirms resolution or reopens the case.
Audit records are retained.
13.3 Unauthorized Technician Report
Consumer receives technician details.
Technician arrives with mismatched identity or no valid assignment.
Consumer does not authorize service.
Consumer reports the incident in the application or support channel.
System records technician, location, request, and time.
System alerts the service provider and platform support.
The technician account may be temporarily restricted according to policy.
Authorized staff investigate.
The outcome is recorded.
Consumer receives an update.
